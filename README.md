# JackyElectronic-BusinessApp

Business website for **Jacky Electronic** – Electronics & Repair Centre, Gaya.
Sells coolers, fans, induction cookers, mixers, irons and wiring material, and repairs TVs, ACs, refrigerators, washing machines, laptops and mobiles.

## Sections
Home, About, Services (price table and repair services), Manager dashboard (9 KPIs, bar chart, period filter, stock-out list), Track repair, Contact / enquiry (with embedded Google Map).

## Interactive feature
Repair tracking: enter `EF-1024`, `EF-1025`, `EF-1026` or `EF-1027`. The enquiry form opens the customer's email app with the details filled in. The dashboard has a period filter.

## Hyperlinks and their business purpose
| Link | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home → Services / Track repair | Move visitors toward prices and repair status |
| Services → Enquiry form | Turn price browsing into a quote or repair booking |
| Phone (`tel:`) | One-tap calling on mobile |
| WhatsApp | Quick quotes and photos of faulty devices |
| Email (`mailto:`) | Formal enquiries and bulk wiring quotes |
| Google Maps (link and embedded map) | Help customers find the shop |
| BIS website | Customers verify ISI marks for safe products |
